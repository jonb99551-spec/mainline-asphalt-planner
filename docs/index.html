Built for paving crews

Mainline: Asphalt Planner

Plan the run, keep trucks moving, and stay on yield — from the first station to the final daily report.

What it does

Station-to-station planning

Ascending or descending stationing in standard 00+00 notation, with cumulative target tons at every 100-foot mark.

Truck-cycle planning

Uses paver speed, spread rate, plant distance, and truck capacity to calculate required truck count for paving and milling.

Live yield correction

Enter current station and actual tons laid. Mainline computes the adjusted spread rate needed to finish on target.

End-of-day report

Delivered tons, waste, placed tons, waste percentage, gross yield, and final waste-adjusted spread rate — shareable in one tap.

Fully offline

No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. Saved plans live only on your device.

iPhone-native

Built with SwiftUI for iOS 17 and later. Portrait iPhone with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support.
