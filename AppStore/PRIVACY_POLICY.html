Mainline Asphalt Planner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Mainline Asphalt Planner ("Mainline," "we," "us") does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technology, or external server connection.

Information stored on your device

Job names, locations, notes, planning inputs, production values, and saved reports are stored locally on your device inside the app's Application Support folder. This information is used only to provide the app's planning and reporting features and never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Sharing

If you tap the Share button, Apple's system share sheet lets you send a report or plan through an app or service you select (for example, Messages, Mail, or Files). Mainline does not receive, store, or transmit anything about what you share or with whom.

Permissions

Mainline does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, health data, or any other sensitive iOS permission.

Network use

Mainline does not make any network requests. The app is fully offline.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved plans from inside the app at any time. Removing the app from your device also removes its locally stored data, subject to your iCloud or device-level backup settings, which are controlled by iOS and not by Mainline.

Children

Mainline is a professional planning tool and does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other users.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated. The effective date above will be revised accordingly.
