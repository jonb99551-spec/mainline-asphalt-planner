Mainline Privacy Policy

Effective date: Replace before publishing

Mainline: Asphalt Planner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technology, or external server connection.

Information stored on your device

Job names, locations, notes, planning inputs, production values, and saved reports are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s planning and reporting features.

Sharing

If you choose the Share button, Apple’s system share sheet lets you send report text through an app or service you select. Mainline does not receive or retain that shared information.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved plans inside the app. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children

Mainline does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.
